The science in the app

We work hard to make everything in the app accurate and up to date. Facts come from NASA, ESA and other trusted sources, and the sky, the planets and the moons follow real orbits and real time. Some scenes, such as the surface of a planet around another star or the space near a black hole, are science-based pictures of what it might look like; the app labels them. Science changes as we learn more. If you find a mistake, please tell us at lee_hantae@msn.com and we will fix it in an update.
