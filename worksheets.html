SpaceWise worksheets

Printable worksheets for lessons with SpaceWise, one for each curriculum topic. Each sheet has questions for pupils to answer during or after a turn in the headset. The answers for the teacher are at the end of each sheet.

Every number on the sheets comes from the same sources as the app (NASA's planetary fact sheets, checked 3 October 2026). Pupils can check them on the fact cards in the app.

How to use them

  1. Turn on school mode (Grown-ups > School mode). See the guide for teachers.
  2. Start the lesson on the headset. The sheet names the mission, Journey or Science Lab experiment to choose.
  3. The pupil in the headset calls out what they see. The rest of the group writes it down.
  4. After each turn, use Grown-ups > Class > Reset for the next pupil.
  5. Finish with Grown-ups > Class > Learning summary and its "Talk about these" list.

Print in black and white. The sheets have no pictures, so they print on any printer.
