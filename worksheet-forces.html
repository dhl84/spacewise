Curriculum (England, Year 5 Forces):

  • unsupported objects fall towards the Earth because of gravity,
  • air resistance, water resistance and friction,
  • levers, pulleys and gears let a smaller force have a greater effect.

Answers for the teacher

  1. On the Moon.
  2. The value the app shows. A jump on the Moon is about six times higher than the same jump on the Earth.
  3. Jupiter.
  4. About 6 times (9.81 ÷ 1.62 is about 6.1).
  5. The hammer. Air resistance slows the light, wide feather much more.
  6. They land together.
  7. The Moon has no air, so there is no air resistance. Gravity pulls both down in the same way.
  8. The order the app shows. Ask the group why the floors differ: rough or smooth, and how hard gravity presses the box down.
  9. Friction.
  10. Through air.
  11. What it falls through. Water pushes back more than air (water resistance).
  12. With a lever, a pulley or gears, a small force moving a long way lifts a heavy load a short way.
  13. Distance: you pull or turn further for the same lift.
