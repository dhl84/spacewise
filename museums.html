SpaceWise for museums, libraries and science centres

Kiosk mode runs SpaceWise on its own for visitors who take turns. Staff choose the experience and the visit length once. After each visit the app resets by itself and keeps nothing of the visitor.

How a visit works

  1. The start screen names the experience and the visit length, and has one big Start button. Behind it, a calm loop shows the real Earth from orbit, and it can also show the Moon, Saturn and Jupiter.
  2. The visitor starts with one tap of the trigger, a button or a pinch.
  3. The visitor gets what the staff chose: one narrated Journey, a guided view or free Explore. The guided views are Earth from orbit, Saturn and its rings, the Apollo 11 landing site and Jezero Crater on Mars.
  4. One minute before the end, a short note says so. At the end, a calm card thanks the visitor and asks them to take the headset off gently.
  5. The app resets for the next visitor. If the headset comes off, the app also resets. If nothing happens for the idle time that the staff chose, it resets too.

What staff choose

  • What visitors get: a Journey, a guided view or free Explore.
  • The visit length: 3, 5, 10 or 15 minutes.
  • The idle time: 1, 2, 3 or 5 minutes.
  • The reading depth for visitors, from Quick Look to Expert.
  • The start screen: a calm tour or Earth only.
  • A staff PIN of 4 to 6 digits. Staff leave kiosk mode with a 5-second hold and the PIN.

Nothing of a visitor is kept

Each visitor plays on a fresh explorer, and the app deletes it after the visit, with any photos it took. There are no names, no accounts, no ads and no chat. Everything stays on the headset. The privacy policy lists the data that the app collects.

What a headset needs

  • A Meta Quest 3 or 3S, signed in to a Meta account that owns SpaceWise. The app checks this at each start. Each headset account needs its own copy of the app.
  • Meta's own device management changed in 2026. Before you set up many headsets, test one headset with your setup and read Meta's help pages for your licence.
  • Kiosk mode locks the app's own menus. It cannot block the headset's Meta button. Keep a member of staff near the headsets, and keep the Guardian boundary on.
  • Visitors must be 10 or older. Meta's health and safety warnings apply.

Setting it up

  1. Open the tablet, then Settings > Grown-ups, and pass the grown-ups check.
  2. Choose Kiosk mode.
  3. On the Visit tab, choose what visitors get, the visit length, the idle time and the reading depth.
  4. On the Start tab, choose the start screen and set the staff PIN.
  5. Press Start kiosk mode.
