Open licences

SpaceWise uses pictures, maps and data that their makers share under open licences. This page lists every one of them that is under a Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY), Open Government Licence (OGL) or Open Data Commons Attribution (ODC-By) licence, with a link to the original and a note of what we changed.

These third-party files keep their own licences. The store terms and our terms do not limit your rights under those licences. You may copy, share and adapt them under the terms of their licences, which are linked below. You do not need the app to get them: follow the link to the original, or download the file as the app uses it from this website when it is offered here.

The app's own code, text, sounds and art are not covered by this page.
